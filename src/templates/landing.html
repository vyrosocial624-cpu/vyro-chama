01 About the group

A shared habit.
A stronger future.

Vyro is a member-led self help group built around saving together. Members contribute savings according to the group's agreed process, creating a shared fund that can support loans to members.

The group sets its own contribution, loan, and repayment rules. Members should understand and agree to the terms before saving or borrowing.

03 Loan rules

Know the requirements

Borrow with
clear expectations.

These are the current portal eligibility checks. A request still needs guarantor consent, administrator review, and available group funds.

01

Membership

Be an active member for at least three months.

02

Savings-based limit

The maximum request is the lower of three times your recorded positive net savings or the limit set by the group administrator.

03

Meeting attendance

Attend at least 75% of recorded group meetings during your membership.

04

Two guarantors

Select two different approved group members. Both must accept before the request goes to the administrator.

05

Existing loans

Repay your current loan before another request can be considered. One active loan at a time.

06

Approval and repayment

Eligibility does not guarantee approval. Agree to the written amount, repayment schedule, and disclosed charges before accepting a loan.

The group administrator confirms the current written rules, available funds, costs, and any late-payment process before a loan is accepted. No interest rate or late fee is stated here.
