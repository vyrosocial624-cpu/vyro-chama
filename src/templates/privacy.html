Privacy notice · Version 2026-11-17

How member information is used

This notice explains the information used by the Vyro Chama member portal, why it is used, and who can access it. Contact the group administrator if you have a question or need a correction.

Information the portal uses

  • Account and contact details: email address, full name, phone number, and account or membership status.
  • Group financial records: savings contributions and withdrawals, loan requests and purpose, approved loans, repayments, and repayment schedules.
  • Group participation: meeting attendance, guarantor selections and responses, announcements, and account notifications.
  • Administrator records: actions administrators take to review membership, manage loan limits, record transactions, and process group activities.

How information is used and shared

  • Your account and contact details are used to manage membership and portal access.
  • Financial and participation records are used to maintain group records, apply the group’s rules, process loan requests, and show members their own account information.
  • Group administrators can access member and group records to carry out their administrative duties.
  • Approved members can see the group member directory. A member whose guarantee is requested can see the loan request information needed to decide whether to accept.
  • The portal uses Supabase for authentication and database services. Information is processed and stored in the Supabase project configured for the group.

Your password is handled by the authentication service and is not shown to group administrators. Do not share your password or sign-in links.

Access, corrections, and retention

Sign in to view the account information available to you. Contact the group administrator to correct a financial record or request help with other personal details. Group financial and membership records may need to be retained for group administration and record-keeping; the administrator can explain how a specific request is handled.

Access to portal data is restricted through authenticated accounts and database access rules. Keep your sign-in details private and sign out on shared devices.

Notice acknowledgment

When requesting portal access, you must confirm that you have read this notice. The notice version and acknowledgment time are recorded with your member profile when the group’s current Supabase setup is installed.

This notice describes the portal’s current use of information. The group should review it whenever the portal or its data practices change.
