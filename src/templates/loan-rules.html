The eligibility checklist

Before you request a loan

  1. Active membership: You must have been an active group member for at least three months.
  2. Savings: Your request cannot exceed three times your recorded positive net savings, or the loan limit set by the group administrator, whichever is lower.
  3. Attendance: You must have attended at least 75% of recorded group meetings during your membership.
  4. Two guarantors: Select two different approved group members. Both must accept before your request reaches the administrator for review.
  5. Existing loans: Repay your current loan before another request can be considered. Only one active loan is allowed at a time.
  6. Group approval and funds: Meeting the checks does not guarantee approval. Requests are reviewed against group rules and available funds.

Read before you sign

Understand your agreement

Before accepting an approved loan, ask for the amount you will receive, total amount to repay, each due date, the repayment schedule, and any interest, fees, or late-payment consequences in writing. Do not rely on an amount that has not been clearly disclosed and agreed.

This page does not set an interest rate, fee, repayment period, or late-payment penalty. Confirm the current terms directly with the group administrator.

i

If a term is missing or unclear, ask the administrator to explain it before signing.
